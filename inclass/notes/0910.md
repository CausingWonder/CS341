### System Composition
1. Aggregation: weaker, has a relationship, are not dependent on each other, car and engine
2. Inheritance: strong, , are dependent on each other, brain and heart
## Inheritance 
public class A extend B
BD->DB
- Make parent(base) before child(derived)
- Delete child(derived) before parent(base)
#### Access Type
- Public: Everything is aware of the base & child is aware that child inherits from base
- Protected: Only the child and its children are aware that they inherit from base
- Private: Only the child is aware that they inherit from the base
Derived classes do not inherit: Constructor/Destructor, Assignment Operator Members, or Private Members.
